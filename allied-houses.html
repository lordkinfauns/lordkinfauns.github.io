Allied Houses

Friends of this site: societies and fellow websites on Scottish history that link to the Lords of Kinfauns, and that we are glad to link to in return.

  • Dunning Parish Historical SocietyThe history of Dunning in Strathearn, Perthshire: its people, its census records and the Dupplin Cross, which now stands in St Serf’s Church. Dupplin gave the 6th Lord Kinfauns his title when he was made Viscount of Dupplin in 1627.
